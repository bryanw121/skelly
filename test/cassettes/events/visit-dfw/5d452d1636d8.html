Events in Plano

Plano, Texas offers a wide range of events, from music concerts, food festivals, and sports competitions to museum displays, exhibits, and family fun. Plan your trip around one of Plano's top annual festivals like the Plano Balloon Festival, AsiaFest, or the Plano International Festival, or book your stay and catch a major sporting event like the Plano Half Marathon. Use our listings of Plano's featured and ongoing events to find the perfect activity for your vacation. For the latest updates about the status of each event, please check each event's website.
