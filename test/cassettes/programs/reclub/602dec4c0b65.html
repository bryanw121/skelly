🎖️ TOURNAMENT FORMAT
Individual Competition – Rotating Partners

• Total participants: 24 players

• Total games: 5 rounds

• The tournament will use a pool-based system.

• Playing partners will change in every round.

• Tennis scoring system (0, 15, 30, 40, Game).

• Every player will have their own individual ranking.

• Winners will be determined based on the total accumulated individual points.

• In case of a tie:

- The player with the highest number of match wins will be ranked higher.

- If the number of match wins is also equal, the player with the higher position on the leaderboard will be ranked higher.

• The Top 3 players with the highest accumulated points will be crowned Individual Champions.
