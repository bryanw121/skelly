🚨 Team Event Format :

🏓 *Team Tie Format*

(Each Team Tie shall consist with following orders. And every player must be occupied.

* 2xMen’s Doubles (MD)
* 1xWomen’s Doubles (WD)
* 3xMixed Doubles (XD)

🏓 *Number of Teams*
* Maximum: 4 teams
* Each team must consist of 6 - 9 players (minimum 4 Male and 2 Female players per team)

🏓 *Scoring Format*
* Round Robin Group Stage : 15-point rally sudden death
* Final: 21-point rally sudden death

* Any DUPR rating/NR/Not Connected can join.
* No DUPR submission during the event.

* Round-robin format in group stage
