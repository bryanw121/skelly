OFFICIAL TOURNAMENT REGULATIONS

1. ⏰ Punctuality Policy
• Players must arrive and complete registration check-in at least 30 minutes before the respective category start time.
• Late arrival may result in disqualification without refund.


2. DUPR ELIGIBILITY

📌 Men's Doubles ≤ 2.800
· Maximum eligible DUPR rating = 2.800
· Reliability ≥ 60%
· NR (No Rating) players are not eligible

📌 Men's Doubles ≤ 3.300
· Maximum eligible DUPR rating = 3.300
· Reliability ≥ 60%
· NR (No Rating) players are not eligible

3. Podium Restriction

• Only podium results officially recorded on SportsSync at the time of eligibility verification will be counted.
• Results from other platforms (Baseline, Reclub, etc) will not be counted.
• Podium finishes include Champion, 1st Runner-up, 2nd Runner-up

📌 Men's Doubles ≤ 2.800
✅ SportSync podium in Men's Doubles ≤ 2.8 or combined ≤ 5.6 will NOT be counted.
✅ Eligible: Maximum 1 SportsSync podium in Men’s Doubles above 2.8, or combined above 5.6.
❌ Not Eligible: 2 or more SportsSync podium in Men’s Doubles above 2.8, or combined above 5.6.

📌 Men's Doubles ≤ 3.300
✅ SportSync podium in Men's Doubles ≤ 3.3 or combined ≤ 6.6 will NOT be counted.
✅ Eligible: Maximum 1 SportsSync podium in Men’s Doubles above 3.3 or combined above 6.6.
❌ Not Eligible: 2 or more SportsSync podium in Men’s Doubles above 3.3 or combined above 6.6.


4. DUPR INTEGRITY, SCREENING & FAIR PLAY

• Match results will NOT be submitted to DUPR.
• Players must self-check eligibility before payment.
• Primary eligibility reference: Official DUPR during registration and verification, up to full payment after approval.
• Skill benchmark: DUPR is not the only indicator of playing level. Relevant Men’s Doubles podium history in SportsSync is a key benchmark of demonstrated competitive performance.
• Suspected manipulation, unusual rating movement or competitive misrepresentation may be reported to the Tournament Director, Happy Karen (000-000-0000).
• A report does not automatically result in disqualification. The Tournament Director may review up to 6 months of DUPR movement and match history.
• The Organiser may reject entry, reassign category, disqualify without refund, remove league points or suspend participation where false information, manipulation or competitive misrepresentation is reasonably identified.
• The Tournament Director’s decision is final.

8. TOURNAMENT STRUCTURE & SCORING FORMAT

📌 Group Stage
• Participant teams will be divided into 4 groups
• Round robin format.
• Rally scoring to 15 points (first to 14 wins)
• No deuce. Sudden death applies at 14–14.
• Switch sides at 8 points
• No timeout allowed
• Top 2 teams from each group will advance to the Knockout Stage.
• Format may be adjusted based on participation level.

📌 Tie-Breaking (Group Stage)
Ranking priority:
Head-to-head
Point differential
Total points scored

📌 Quarterfinals
• Rally scoring to 15 points (first to 15 wins)
• Serve-to-win rule applies at 14–14 (win by 1 point)
• Switch sides at 8 points
• No timeout allowed

📌 Semifinals, 3rd Placement & Finals
• Rally scoring to 21 points (first to 21 wins)
• Serve-to-win rule applies at 20–20 (win by 1 point)
• Switch sides at 11 points
• One 1-minute timeout per team

9. MATCH OFFICIATING
👨‍⚖️ Group Stage to Quarter Finals
• Self-officiated.
• Receiving team makes line and fault calls.
• Disputes should be resolved respectfully.
• The Tournament Director or officials, if present, may overrule a call only if they have a clear and unobstructed view of the play.
• Video recordings will not be accepted as evidence.

👨‍⚖️ Semifinals, 3rd Placement & Finals
• Matches will be officiated by appointed referees.
• Referee and line judges will make final decisions.
• Video recordings will not be accepted as evidence.

• The organiser reserves the right to remove or ban players who violate the rules or display poor sportsmanship.
• Players are expected to compete honestly and respect opponents, partners, officials and fellow participants.
• Abusive behaviour, harassment, intimidation, deliberate cheating or unsportsmanlike conduct may result in a warning, match forfeiture, disqualification or suspension from future Bliss Pickleball Club events.

10. TIMEOUT POLICY
• No timeout allowed for 15-point rally matches.
• One 1-minute timeout per team is allowed for 21-point rally matches.
• Injury timeout: Maximum 10 minutes per team per match.
• Withdrawal applies if player cannot continue.
