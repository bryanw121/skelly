🏆🏓 THE KEYCHAIN CLUB PRESENTS: NOVICE+INTERMEDIATE DOUBLES

THE KEYCHAIN CLASH: DINK WARS EDITION!

It’s time to take your Open Play skills to the next level! We’re bringing friendly competition to the court with our very first mini-tournament!

Think you and your partner have what it takes to claim the title? Let’s find out! 👀

🎾 TOURNAMENT CATEGORY
NOVICE & INTERMEDIATE DOUBLES
(Preferably mixed doubles)
Note: Pairs with either both male or female players will still be subject to assessment and approval.

📌 TOURNAMENT DETAILS
• Doubles Category
• 2 Brackets
• Single Round Robin Format
• Top 2 pairs from each bracket advance to the Semifinals
• Championship & 3rd Place Match

🏓 GAME FORMAT
• Round Robin: First to 11 points, win by 2
• Semifinals & Finals: First to 11 points, win by 2

🏆 AWARDS & PRIZES
• Champion
• 1st Runner-up
• 2nd Runner-up

📝 REGISTRATION
• Registration is on a first-come, first-served basis.
• Register as a pair (find your battle buddy!).
• Slots are limited to 10 pairs only for now.
• Both players must meet the Novice + Intermediate category requirements.

📅 Tournament Date: November 25
📍 Venue: KPC Angono
💰 Registration Fee: 500.00 each player

Ready to rally, compete, and have some fun?

Tag your partner, grab your paddles, and secure your slots!

10 PAIRS. 1 CHAMPION. LET THE DINK WARS BEGIN!

See you on the court, Picklers! ❤️

*EXCLUSIVE to The Keychain Club members and those who have previously joined our Open Play sessions only.
