🏓 1. TEAM REQUIREMENTS

- Each team must consist of 6 players.
- Each team must have at least 4 male players and 2 female player.
- Open to all DUPR ratings, including NR and Not Connected (NC) players.
- No DUPR submission will be made for this event.
- All players must comply with the host's team and rotation arrangements.

⚡ 2. MATCH FORMAT —
Each Team Tie shall consist with following orders. And every player must be occupied.

Team Captain & Player Arrangement

1. Every team must appoint one Captain.

2. The Captain is responsible for arranging and assigning players to the respective match categories:

- Mix Doubles 1 (Mix 1)
- Mix Doubles 2 (Mix 2)
- Men's Doubles 1 (Men 1)
- Men's Doubles 2 (Men 2)
- Women's Doubles (Women 1)

3. Match Order:

1. Mix 1
2. Mix 2
3. Men 1
4. Men 2
5. Women 1

4. The Captain must ensure all players are assigned to the correct categories before the matches begin.

Important: Team Captains are responsible for coordinating their players and ensuring the lineup is ready before each match.


🏓 *Number of Teams*

* Each team must consist of 6 - 8 players (minimum 4 Male and 2 Female players per team)

Group Stage（15 point sudden t death） → top 2 → Semi Final（15 point serve to win）→ Final（21 point serve to ein）
