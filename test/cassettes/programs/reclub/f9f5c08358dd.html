🏓 MOCA PICKLEVILLE – 4TH MONTHSARY

MIXED PUKSAAN MINI TOURNAMENT

A mini tournament filled with friendly competition, exciting matches, and a community gathering!

🏆 TOURNAMENT DETAILS

- Team Composition: 2–3 players per team
- Required Composition: At least one (1) male and one (1) female player per team.
- Optional 3rd Player.
- Registration Fee: ₱100 per player
- Skill Category: Novice to Intermediate
- Maximum Self-Rating: 3.0
- Tournament Format: Pool Play
- Number of Pools: 3 (subject to the number of registered teams and other circumstances)

🏓 TOURNAMENT FORMAT

1. Pool Play

- Three (3) pools will be formed.
- Each pool follows a Single Round Robin format.
- Teams will be randomly assigned to their respective pools.
- Every team plays against all other teams within its assigned pool.
- Matches are First to 11 points.
- No deuce. The first team to reach 11 points wins.

2. Advancement to Quarterfinals

A total of eight (8) teams will advance:

- Top two (2) teams from each pool automatically qualify (6 teams).
- Two (2) best third-place teams across all pools will receive the Wild Card slots.

3. Quarterfinals

- Eight (8) qualifying teams will compete in a knockout round.
- Winners advance to the semifinals.
- Match scoring follows the knockout-stage scoring system below.

4. Semifinals

The four remaining teams will compete for a spot in the championship finals.

- Rank 1 vs. Rank 4
- Rank 2 vs. Rank 3
- First to 11 points, with deuce.
- At 14–14, the first team to reach 15 wins.

5. Battle for 3rd Place and Finals

- Both matches follow the same scoring system as the semifinals.
- First to 11 points, with deuce.
- At 14–14, the first team to reach 15 wins.

IMPORTANT: Tournament format and mechanics may be adjusted depending on the number of registered teams and other unforeseen circumstances.

📋 MATCH RULES

Before the Match

1. Teams will play Bato-Bato-Pick (Rock-Paper-Scissors).
2. The winning team chooses their preferred court side.
3. The losing team will determine the first serving team.

During the Match

1. Teams switch court sides after 6 points.
2. Teams with three (3) players may substitute, but substitutions are optional.
3. Same-Gender Substitution ONLY: Substitutions are allowed only between same gender teammates.
4. Substitutions are allowed ONLY during the court-side change.
5. Player's Call: Players are expected to make honest and fair calls. In case of a dispute, the player nearest to the ball will make the call.

📊 POOL STANDINGS & TIEBREAKERS

In case of tied Win-Loss Records, the following criteria will apply in order:

1. Win-Loss Record
2. Point Differential (Points Scored minus Points Allowed)
3. Total Points Scored
4. Head-to-Head (H2H) Result

🏆 CHAMPIONSHIP PRIZES

🥇 1st Place: ₱600 Cash Prize
🥈 2nd Place: ₱300 Cash Prize
🥉 3rd Place: Regular Open Play (OP) Voucher

🤝 SPORTSMANSHIP & FAIR PLAY

- Players must honestly declare their skill ratings.
- Sandbagging or deliberately understating one's skill level to gain an unfair advantage is prohibited.
- Organizers reserve the right to review player eligibility.
- Players found intentionally misrepresenting their skill level may be disqualified.
- Respect teammates, opponents, organizers, and fellow players.
- Make honest line calls and avoid unnecessary arguments.
- Cheating, intentional manipulation of match results, and unsportsmanlike conduct may result in penalties or disqualification.
- The organizers' decisions on tournament matters shall be respected.

Note: A minimum of 10 participating teams is required to continue the tournament.
