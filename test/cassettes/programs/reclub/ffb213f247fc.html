🎯 WHAT IS PRIME PADEL GAMEWEEK?
Prime Padel Gameweek is a competitive platform that combines the fun of social padel with a structured tournament experience.

Our goal is to give players the opportunity to compete, develop their game, and track their progress through an individual ranking system.

🏆 COMPETE
Experience structured competitive matches and challenge yourself against other players.

🤝 DEVELOP
Test your partner chemistry, discover your strengths, and gain valuable experience before bigger tournaments.

📈 CLIMB
Earn points in every Gameweek and compete for your position on the PPG Leaderboard!

🤔 WHO CAN JOIN?
✅ ELIGIBLE TO JOIN

* Self-Rating 1.0–2.0 — For reference only.
* Beginner Tournament Winners — Eligible to join, including with the same partner.
* Upper Beginner Tournament Winners — Eligible to join with a new partner, provided the pair has never won an Upper Beginner tournament together.
* Regular Beginner & Upper Beginner Players — Players who regularly participate in Beginner and Upper Beginner tournaments.
* Players Who Want to Start Competing — For players looking to experience competitive matches in a friendly environment.
* Test Your Partner Before Bigger Tournaments A semi-tournament concept that combines social play with a competitive format.

❌ NOT ELIGIBLE TO JOIN
* Self-Rating Above 2.0 — Recommended to join a higher category.
* Regular Higher-Level Players — Players who regularly compete at Lower Bronze, Bronze, Silver, or above.
* Upper Beginner Champion Pairs — Pairs who have previously won an Upper Beginner tournament together.
* Home-Court / Padel Coach Players — Recommended to join Lower Bronze – Bronze.
* Lower Bronze Semifinalists — Players who ever have reached the semifinals or higher in a Lower Bronze tournament.
* Professional Players / Athletes — Not eligible for this category.

📖 PLAYER CURATION
To maintain fair, balanced, and competitive matches, all registrations are subject to review by the event committee.

The committee reserves the right to review player levels and pairings to ensure fair competition for everyone.
