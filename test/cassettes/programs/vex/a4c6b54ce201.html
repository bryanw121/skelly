Who we are

Vex Volleyball is an adult volleyball community focused on effort, development, and quality play.

We believe players get better through organized, team-based volleyball — not chaos or rigid gatekeeping.

Our Koi, Jade, and Dragon divisions guide expectations while allowing players to:

  • play hard

  • try new positions

  • play with friends of different levels

  • and grow at their own pace

Whether you’re new to the sport or chasing competitive reps, Vex exists to give adults meaningful playtime, clear structure, and a culture built on effort and respect.
