About this event

Description

Join us for Open Play Pickleball, a fun and welcoming drop-in session for players of all skill levels! Whether you're brand new to the game or a seasoned player looking for friendly competition, our courts are open for casual matches, social play, and skill-building. Equipment is available for those who need it, and all ages and abilities are welcome. Come stay active, meet new people, and enjoy one of the fastest‑growing sports around!
