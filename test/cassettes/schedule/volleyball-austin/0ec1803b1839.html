Come play with Volleyball Austin at our Open Play!

We will have 1 court for Lower (social) and 2 courts for Upper (skilled) level play. For the safety and enjoyment of all players on the court, please play on the court that is the best fit for you. If it is determined that your skillset and knowledge is not aligned with a particular court, you will be kindly asked to move to the appropriate court. Board members reserve the right to adjust the level of play on each court based on volume and skill level of players present to accommodate maximum playing time and the best player experience. 

The 2 Upper courts will share one sign-up sheet and the Lower court will have its' own sign-up sheet. You may not sign up anyone except yourself and you may not sign up twice at the same time.

We'll begin with warm ups and Queen/King of the Court. The hosts will then announce for games to begin and the score maximum per set (15, 21 or 25). We kindly ask those waiting for their next game to keep score with the scoreboards by each court. 

Please ensure you pay online prior to expedite check-in as there are no payment terminals onsite. If we reach capacity and you are placed on the waitlist, you will need to wait 15 minutes after start time and be present in order to claim a potential spot. 

Feel free to reach out to the Board with any questions by contacting redacted@example.com. A board member will also be present to welcome you and answer any questions at check-in. Enjoy your time on the court! :)
