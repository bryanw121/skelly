      Winter, Ice Rodeo

      Discover one of the top holiday activities in Austin at Ice Rodeo. This festive winter experience features lakeside ice skating, seasonal dining, holiday décor and unforgettable family fun downtown.
      November 20, 2026 – January 3, 2027

      Ice Rodeo Winter Activities

      Ice Skating | Daily, 4:00 pm – 9:00 pm
      Book Now
      The shining bulb on the Hotel’s holiday tree is the 3,360 square foot (312 square metre) outdoor ice-skating rink. Skaters can glide beneath the Texas sky on the shores of Lady Bird Lake on a rink – built atop the Hotel pool – composed of real ice that is cooled by state-of-the-art chillers.
      Rinkside Dining Cabins | Daily, 4:00 pm – 9:00 pm
      Book Now
      For a more private experience, reserve one of the rinkside cabins: lodge-inspired retreats designed for up to six guests. Each cabin offers a cozy, festive setting complete with plush seating, seasonal décor and personalized service. Available daily, these exclusive spaces provide the perfect backdrop for holiday gatherings with friends and family or an intimate evening for two.
      Gingerbread Display The décor turns delicious as guests head indoors for a peek at the Hotel’s famous annual gingerbread lobby display created by Executive Pastry Chef Amanda Pallagi and team. This year’s display celebrates the Hotel’s 40th Anniversary, with homage to four distinct decades in the city. Those looking to share the love and support cancer research can have their name added to the display with all proceeds benefiting Dell Children’s Foundation.
      Holiday Candy Shop | Daily, 4:00 pm – 9:00 pm Satisfy your sweet tooth at our rinkside Candy Shop, filled with handcrafted chocolates, nostalgic confections and house-made treats – perfect for gifting or indulging in between skates.

      Festive Dining

      Hidden Flask Speakeasy | Thursdays – Saturdays, 5:00 pm – 9:00 pm
      Book Now
      Tucked behind closed doors every Thursday to Saturday, the Hidden Flask offers an intimate speakeasy experience—available for Hotel guests and locals alike—layered with mystery and allure. The intimate hideaway, which transforms into a Winter Wonderland, offers an evening of warmth and discovery, complete with a fireplace-lined deck, holiday inspired cocktails and a menu of festive bites designed to pair seamlessly with the season’s spirits.
      Teddy Bear Brunch | November 29 and December 13
      Book Now
      Gather with family and friends for a festive holiday experience featuring an elevated brunch buffet, seasonal cocktails and beverages, whimsical teddy bear décor, and a special visit from Santa Claus. Create cherished holiday memories while enjoying a joyful atmosphere designed to delight guests of all ages. In the spirit of giving, guests are encouraged to bring a new, unwrapped toy or teddy bear to help brighten the holidays for local children. Donations will benefit Dell Children’s Hospital and The SAFE Alliance.
      Holiday A-Tea-X | Daily, 1:00 pm – 3:00 pm
      Book Now
      Embrace the magic of the season with a cozy afternoon of festive indulgence. Enjoy tea-inspired cocktails, comforting teas and seasonal bites infused with rich, holiday flavours. Gather at Live Oak for a laid-back experience of sipping, savouring and celebrating the spirit of winter with friends.
      Christmas Brunch
      Book Now
      Celebrate Christmas Day at Four Seasons Hotel Austin. Gather with family and friends for an elegant Christmas Brunch featuring seasonal favourites, chef-curated brunch specialties, decadent desserts and holiday beverages in the festive lake-level ballroom. Enjoy live holiday entertainment, joyful seasonal décor and memorable moments with loved ones as you celebrate one of the year's most cherished traditions.
      The Deck Pop-Up Lounge | Daily, 5:00 pm – 9:00 pm Enjoy a curated menu of specialty cocktails, champagne, caviar and light bites in a winter-chic setting with views of the 30 foot Christmas tree from the Live Oak Deck.
      Ring in the New Year at Hidden Flask
      Book Now
      Step behind closed doors into an unforgettable New Year’s Eve celebration at Hidden Flask, the Hotel’s intimate seasonal speakeasy. Gather around the fireplace-lined deck for firework views and raise a glass to the New Year with a midnight Dom Perignon toast.
      Celebrate New Year’s Eve at Live Oak
      Book Now
      Gather with friends and loved ones to ring in the New Year at Live Oak. Enjoy live entertainment, passed hors d’oeuvres and festive favours throughout the night and make the countdown memorable with a champagne toast and ball drop at the stroke of midnight.

      Winter Experiences

      Holiday Décor Marvel in the magic as holiday delights are on full display across the entire property. After an arrival beneath a blanket of thousands of twinkling lights and then gallivanting through a 120-foot (37 metre) tunnel of lighted stars, guests can spot elaborate Texas-themed art installations that have been cleverly tucked across property for the perfect photo opportunity. Of course, no visit is complete without a twirl beside the 30-foot (9 metre) Christmas Tree located on the back lawn.
      Sound Healing Wellness Series | November 22, December 27 9:00 am – 10:00 am
      Book Now
      Enjoy monthly wellness offerings to rejuvenate the body and mind with local wellness expert, ATX Yoga Girl for a sound healing class invites stillness, harmony and inner calm. All wellness series events are open to the public at a cost of USD 35 and are complimentary for Hotel guests.
      Winter Solstice Journey
      Details
      Embrace the quiet beauty of the season with this restorative winter ritual. Warm citrus and clove aromatherapy ease tension and calm the nervous system, while red-light therapy enhances natural radiance. A revitalizing exfoliating foot treatment completes this journey of warmth, renewal and balance.

      Austin Holiday Escape

      Experience the magic of the holidays from the heart of Austin. Overlooking Lady Bird Lake, Four Seasons Hotel Austin pairs festive seasonal experiences with award-winning dining, a serene spa, luxury accommodations and world-class service. After exploring Ice Rodeo and the city’s holiday events, unwind lakeside while enjoying effortless access to Austin’s vibrant music, shopping and cultural scene.

      Premier Lake-View Balcony Room

      Drink in warm sunshine and fresh winter breezes from your private balcony, which looks out to the best holiday “backyard” in Austin: our manicured lawns, verdant gardens and Lady Bird Lake.
