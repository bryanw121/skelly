FAQ

When is the ice rink active?

The Ice Rink at the Long Center is active from November 21, 2026 – January 4, 2027, weekdays from 3pm to 10pm, and 10am to 10pm on weekends and holidays. 

How much does it cost?

All-in tickets range from $25 – $33 and include skate rentals and 60 minutes of ice time.  

When can I get my tickets?

You can purchase tickets online ahead of time or on-site (first come, first served). Please note that your reservation is valid only for the date and time you select.  After purchase, you’ll receive a confirmation email and your mobile ticket delivery 3 days prior to your reservation.

Can I bring food and drinks?

No, outside food and drink are not allowed. Festive beverages & snacks are available to purchase at the ice rink.

Can I come to watch without buying a ticket?

Yes, spectators are allowed to visit the Ice Rink without a ticket.  

Do Long Center Members get a discount?

Long Center Members get a 20% discount on ice rink tickets. Check your email for the code!
