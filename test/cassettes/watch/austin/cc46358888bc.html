Glide and twirl under the twinkling lights on the largest ice skating rink in Central Texas. Whether you're a pro or a beginner, the rink is perfect for everyone to enjoy the cool, crisp air and holiday tunes.

This class is perfect for beginners and those new to the ice! Our experienced instructors will guide your child through fundamental skating skills and help build confidence with every glide.

Each lesson includes both instruction time and a free skate period, allowing skaters to practice independently or receive additional guidance from the instructor. Lessons take place before the rink opens to the public, providing a calm and supportive environment for learning.

We recommend arriving 30 minutes early to allow time for parking, check-in, and getting fitted for skates. Helmets and knee pads are strongly recommended for children ages 10 and under.
